WHO WE ARE

A non-profit built on community action.

InAmigos Foundation is a grassroots non-profit working to empower communities across India through targeted programs in education, health, environment, women empowerment, animal welfare and skill development. Every initiative begins with listening to the people we serve — understanding local needs, co-creating solutions, and building ownership that outlasts any single project.

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). The organization is a Section 8 registered non-profit based in Chhattisgarh.
