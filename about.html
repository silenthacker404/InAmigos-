OUR STORY

From a small idea to widespread community action.

What began as a single act of care has grown into a movement connecting volunteers, donors and communities across India.

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). The organization is a Section 8 registered non-profit based in Chhattisgarh.

According to the organization's website, the foundation works across multiple causes including education, women empowerment, animal welfare, community support, environment and skill development.
